## What I'd Do About It

This week, the dominant theme is AI, with a clear emphasis on AI agents and their evolving capabilities. OpenAI continues to push the envelope, demonstrating AI's application in education with active learning support and expanding agent utility beyond coding through new models like GPT-6 Astra and Ultrafast. We're seeing AI agents drive longer video creation and maintain focus for extended periods in research, even enhancing their performance over time.

However, a critical observation from Hacker News highlights a disconnect: while AI coding agents generate more code, this doesn't automatically translate to more *software*. NVIDIA's perspective on AI agents transforming software development from manual coding to agent direction underscores this shift. For engineering teams, the immediate action is to evaluate current development workflows. Identify areas where AI agents can genuinely augment, not just automate, the process. Focus on directing agents to solve specific problems rather than merely generating code. The goal is to leverage these tools to build more robust, functional software, not just increase code volume.
